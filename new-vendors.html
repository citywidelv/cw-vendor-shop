City Wide Vendor Resource Center

Become a City Wide Vendor

City Wide Facility Solutions manages the full service budget for commercial facilities across Las Vegas, Northern Nevada, and beyond. Joining the vendor network costs nothing, and City Wide guarantees vendor payments even when property owners pay late. Three steps get your crew eligible for work.

Start with Step 1
1

Tell Us What Your Crew Does

Vendor Evaluation

Fill out the vendor evaluation once and pick every service your crew offers: janitorial, floor care, landscaping, the trades, restoration, and more. When work opens that matches what you do, you hear from us first. It takes about five minutes.

Complete the Vendor Evaluation
2

Meet the Team

30 Minutes at Our Office

A relaxed half hour to get to know each other. No sales pitch and nothing to sign. We explain how City Wide works, the types of properties we manage, and the kinds of opportunities that become available, and we answer any questions you have about working with us.

  • Booking opens our Microsoft Bookings calendar, which syncs with our Operations team calendars, so every time you see is actually available. Pick one that works for you and come by the office.
  • Bring anything you want to show us: examples of your work, business cards, photos of past jobs, anything that tells us about your company.
  • Pick the meeting that matches your trade when you book. You leave knowing exactly how work gets assigned and what happens next.

Do janitorial? Use the janitorial packet.

It covers everything, so crews that do janitorial plus other services fill out one packet, not two. The difference: janitorial crews hold keys and often work in buildings alone, so their packet adds agreements on building security, key and access responsibility, and how janitorial work runs. The packet walks you through each one.
