4

Banking: Voided Check or Bank Letter

Direct Deposit Setup▼

Your packet includes an ACH authorization so we can pay you by direct deposit. We verify the account with one of the two documents below. Upload yours inside the packet, or email it to LVCompliance@gocitywide.com for Las Vegas or rncompliance@gocitywide.com for Northern Nevada if it is not ready when you sign.

Option 1: Voided Business Check

A printed check from your business checking account with VOID written across the front. The business name printed on the check must match your ACH authorization letter for letter. Starter checks, counter checks, and deposit slips cannot be accepted.

Option 2: Bank Letter

No printed checks? Ask your bank for an account verification letter. Most banks produce one the same day at a branch or through online support. It must include everything on the checklist below.

A valid bank letter has all five:

  • Printed on the bank's letterhead. Online banking screenshots and generic printouts cannot be accepted.
  • Shows your business legal name as the account holder, exactly as it reads on your ACH authorization.
  • Shows the full routing number and account number, and states the account type.
  • Signed by a bank employee, with their name, title, and contact information.
  • Dated within the last 90 days.
The rule that matters most

The name on your check or bank letter, your ACH authorization, and your packet must all be the same business name, and it must be a business account. We pay your company, not you personally. A personal account or a name that does not match stops payment setup until it is fixed.

Done when your check or bank letter is uploaded or emailed in.

The Five Things That Slow Vendors Down

Nearly every onboarding delay is one of these five. Dodge them all and you are on file and eligible for work in days.

  • A personal name on the banking. The check, the bank letter, and the ACH authorization all need your company name. We pay businesses, not individuals.
  • Names that almost match. Desert Cleaning on the check and Desert Cleaning Group LLC on the packet sends us back to you. Letter for letter, everywhere.
  • Starter checks and stale letters. Temporary checks, screenshots, and bank letters older than 90 days cannot be accepted.
  • A certificate naming the wrong entity. City Wide alone is not enough. Your agent needs the exact certificate holder shown on the request form.
  • Signing the waiver early. The notary must watch you sign. A waiver signed at the kitchen table has to be redone.
