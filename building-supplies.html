Order Building Supplies

Tell us what the building needs: paper, soap, liners, air care, and restroom stock. No brands or part numbers required; we match the right product to the building's dispensers and handle the ordering. These are the customer's supplies: City Wide orders them for the building and invoices the customer, never you. Crew equipment, chemicals, and uniforms live in the Vendor Shop.

How this works: pick what the building goes through and tell us roughly how much. You are not buying anything and nothing is ever charged to you. These are the customer's building supplies: City Wide orders them on the customer's behalf and the customer is invoiced directly. We match each item to the building's dispensers and confirm pack and case sizes by email. Unsure which refill fits? Reply to your confirmation email with photos of the dispensers and we will match them.
