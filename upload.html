Upload Insurance & Compliance Documents

Certificate of insurance (new or a renewal), notarized Waiver of Agreement, or your voided check or bank letter: send it here and it goes straight to the City Wide compliance team for your region. Vendors and insurance agents both welcome. You get a confirmation copy for your records.

Sending a certificate of insurance? It must name the exact City Wide entity for your region, letter for letter. Your COI request form (from the New Vendor Onboarding page) shows exactly what the certificate needs to include.
