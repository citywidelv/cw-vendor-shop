Submit Your Monthly Invoice

Already have an invoice from your own system? Upload it. Need one? Build it right here. Either way, City Wide Accounts Payable gets it the moment you hit submit, and you get a confirmation copy for your records. Payment terms are Net 10th Prox: due the 10th of the month after your invoice.

How would you like to submit your invoice?

Both go straight to City Wide Accounts Payable. Pick whichever is easier for you.

Quick check: make sure your invoice shows the month of service, each account you served, and the Work Order number from the FSM for any extra charge or project work. Missing Work Order numbers can slow down payment.
When You Get Paid

Per your Master Service Agreement, payment terms are Net 10th Prox: payment goes out on the 10th of the month following confirmed service. Recurring contract work pays automatically. For one-time projects and extra work, we first confirm the job is complete, either by visiting the site or by hearing from the customer that they are satisfied, and payment then lands on the following 10th.
