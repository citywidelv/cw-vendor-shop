Why a Starting Kit Matters

The fastest way to lose a new account is to show up under equipped. One towel and one mop head cannot clean a building, and it shows on night one. This planner sets the minimum inventory to load on the van before the first shift.

The 3x Rotation Rule

Every pad and cloth quantity below is tripled on purpose: one set in use tonight, one set in the laundry, and one clean spare on the shelf. If you only buy one set, you are choosing between skipping the laundry and skipping the cleaning.

    Where the Numbers Come From

    These quantities are grounded in ISSA production standards and City Wide field experience, not guesswork:

    • Flat mop pads: change the pad about every 500 to 750 sq ft of hard floor. Mopping past that point pushes dirty water around instead of picking it up.
    • Microfiber cloths: retire a cloth when you finish a zone, roughly one cloth per 250 to 300 sq ft of surface work. Restrooms burn cloths faster.
    • Team model: quantities assume the City Wide 4 specialist team approach (light duty, vacuum, restroom, and utility) on larger buildings.

    Before You Load the Van

    • Kit matches the table above for this building size and type
    • Every cloth and pad color is stocked: red, yellow, blue, and green
    • Chemicals are labeled, diluted correctly, and spray bottles are marked
    • Vacuum runs, filter is clean, and a spare belt and bags are packed
    • Wet floor signs, gloves, and PPE are in the vehicle
    • Laundry plan is confirmed: who washes, where, and by when
    • Building keys, alarm codes, and the scope of work are in hand
